What makes us different

Built for how Indian businesses actually sell

IndiaMART, Justdial & TradeIndia sync

WhatsApp built in

Send quotes, catalogues, payment links and order updates on WhatsApp from inside the lead or order — every chat saved on the customer record.

Calling & telecaller mode

Click-to-call, auto-dialler, call recording and disposition codes for telecalling teams, using your SIM or cloud telephony.

Field force & distributor orders

Support linked to orders

Every ticket shows the customer's orders, invoices and warranty — so your support team fixes issues without asking the same questions twice.
