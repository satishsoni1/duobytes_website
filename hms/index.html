Why hospitals switch

From files and registers to a paperless hospital

Without an HMS

  • Long reception queues and handwritten case papers
  • Patient history lost between OPD, IPD and lab files
  • Missed charges — services given but never billed
  • Pharmacy stock and expiry tracked in notebooks
  • Lab reports printed, collected and re-typed
  • TPA claims delayed by missing documents
  • Owner has no live view of beds, revenue or doctors

With DuoBytes HMS

  • Online booking, token display and 1-minute registration
  • Single EMR with history, prescriptions and reports
  • Every service auto-posted to the patient bill
  • Pharmacy with batch, expiry and reorder alerts
  • Lab machines interfaced, reports sent on WhatsApp
  • TPA pre-auth and claim documents in one click
  • Live dashboards for occupancy, revenue and doctor-wise OPD
