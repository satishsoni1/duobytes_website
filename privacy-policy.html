Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

This policy explains how DuoBytes Technologies Pvt Ltd ("DuoBytes", "we", "us") collects, uses and protects personal information when you visit our websites (duobytes.in and its product pages for School ERP, Sales ERP, HMS and FFRS) or contact us.

1. Information we collect

  • Information you give us: name, organisation, phone number, email address and any details you enter in our demo or enquiry forms, or send us on WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • Information collected automatically: pages visited, device and browser type, approximate location, referring website and campaign parameters (such as UTM tags), collected through cookies and similar technologies.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries, schedule demos and provide quotations.
  • To provide, support and improve our products and services.
  • To measure website performance and the effectiveness of our advertising.
  • To send relevant product updates, which you can opt out of at any time.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Cookies, analytics and advertising

We use Google Analytics, Google Ads, Meta (Facebook/Instagram) Pixel and may use Microsoft Clarity and LinkedIn Insight Tag. These services use cookies to understand how visitors use our site and to show our ads to people who have visited it (remarketing). You can control cookies through your browser settings, opt out of Google ads personalisation at adssettings.google.com, and manage Meta ad preferences in your Facebook/Instagram settings.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate our business (for example hosting, analytics, communication and advertising platforms), under appropriate confidentiality obligations, or when required by law.

5. Customer data in our products

Data that schools, hospitals and businesses store in our software (for example student, patient or customer records) belongs to that customer. We process it only to provide the service, under our agreement with that customer, and keep it secure with encryption, access controls and backups.

6. Data retention and security

We keep enquiry information for as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect it.
